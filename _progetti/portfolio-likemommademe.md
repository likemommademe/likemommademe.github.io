concept_en: |
  A portfolio that didn't look like a template. Bold in its headlines, playful in the details and crystal clear when you need to find a project.

  The name is already a joke: *like mamma made me*. The site plays along from start to finish, from the winking eye in the motto to the 7 hidden in place of the T in THE.
execution: |
  L’ho progettato e costruito interamente con **Claude** dentro **Visual Studio Code**, senza template e senza page builder:

  - uno **showreel in codice** che fa da header e si muove con lo scroll: cinque mondi e un finale a sorpresa;
  - le **pagine progetto** a bande, con le gallerie a coppie e i numeri «dietro le quinte»;
  - i **lavori in scorrimento orizzontale** in home, il **sottomenu** di Works e il tema giorno e notte;
  - un **pannello su misura** per aggiornare tutto da solo: progetti, gallerie, testi in italiano e inglese, accesso con Google.

  Io ho deciso cosa costruire e come doveva apparire; Claude ha scritto il codice con me, passo dopo passo.
execution_en: |
  I designed and built it entirely with **Claude** in **Visual Studio Code**, with no templates and no page builders:

  - a **showreel in code** that works as the header and moves with the scroll: five worlds and a surprise ending;
  - **project pages** in bands, with paired galleries and "behind the scenes" numbers;
  - **horizontal-scrolling work** on the homepage, the Works **submenu** and day and night themes;
  - a **custom CMS** to update everything myself: projects, galleries, copy in Italian and English, Google sign-in.

  I decided what to build and how it should look; Claude wrote the code with me, step by step.
